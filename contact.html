Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, ideas or copyright requests. Every message is read by the person who makes Makimedia.

Your message is sent to our inbox through Web3Forms. We use your email address only to reply to you. See the privacy policy.

Message sent

Thanks for writing. We'll reply to the email address you gave, usually within a few days.

Prefer email?

Write to support@makimedia.org. It reaches the same inbox.

Bugs and code

Makimedia is open source. You can also report bugs or follow the work on GitHub.

Before you ask

Many answers are already on the Help page. Makimedia is a player, not a streaming service, so we can't help find, download or share films or shows. Read the legal notice.
