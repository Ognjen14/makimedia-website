Last updated 27 September 2026

In short: Makimedia is a video player. It doesn't provide, sell, host or stream any films or shows, and it isn't connected to any streaming service. It plays video files you already have, and you are responsible for having the right to use them.

A player, not a streaming service

Makimedia is free, open source software made by TopicDev for Windows, Android and Android TV. It organises and plays video files that are already stored on your own devices and drives.

  • Makimedia has no catalogue of films or shows, and it doesn't provide, sell, rent or give away any.
  • It doesn't host video or stream anything over the internet. When you stream from your PC, the video goes directly between your own devices on your own home network.
  • It doesn't find, download or share videos, and it has no feature for doing so.
  • It is not affiliated with Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or any other streaming service, and doesn't connect to them.

You choose which files Makimedia plays and streams. Only use it with videos you own or otherwise have the right to watch and copy, and follow the law where you live. TopicDev has no control over, and no knowledge of, the files anyone plays with Makimedia.

Source code

Makimedia's own code is public on GitHub, for every version that is released.

The libraries it includes, mpv and FFmpeg, are used unmodified, in these builds:

  • Windows: mpv 0.41.0-923-g7b8915bc1 with FFmpeg 1d7b14f61, from mpv-winbuild-cmake 20260814

For three years from the release of each version, we will give anyone the complete corresponding source code for these libraries, for no more than what it costs us to send it. To ask for it, email support@makimedia.org or use the contact form with the topic Source code request, and say which version and device you need it for.
