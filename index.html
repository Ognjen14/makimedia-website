Your film library,
on every screen.

Makimedia turns the video folders on your PC into a library with posters, details and progress, then streams it to your Android phone, tablet and TV over your home network. They find the PC on their own, with no server to set up, and the video never leaves your home.

A library made from your own files

Point Makimedia at your folders. It finds the films and shows, fetches their posters, and remembers where you stopped.

Your files come first

The library is built from your folders and works with the network off. A file that can't be identified still plays and still shows up.

Posters and details

Films and shows are matched from their file names, with artwork, cast and episode lists. If a guess isn't sure, Makimedia asks you before using it, and you can always fix a match.

Continue watching

Every film and episode resumes where you left it, and what you're partway through waits at the top of Home.

Collections and universes

Sagas are grouped for you, from Marvel and DC to Spider-Man and Batman, in story order where it matters. You can make your own collections too.

Stream from your PC

Turn on streaming on Windows and your phone, tablet or TV finds it on your home network. Watch the PC's whole library with no setup and no addresses to type.

Plays almost anything

Built on mpv, with hardware decoding up to 4K HEVC. Choose audio and subtitle tracks, adjust their timing, or find subtitles on OpenSubtitles.

Made for each screen

A mouse, a touchscreen and a TV remote each get their own controls. Nothing is squeezed to fit.

A player, not a streaming service

Makimedia plays video files you already have. It has no catalogue of its own, and it doesn't sell, rent, host or stream any films or shows.

What Makimedia does

  • Plays video files stored on your own PC, phone, tablet or drives
  • Streams them only between your own devices, on your own home network
  • Automatically fetches posters and metadata from TMDB for your files

What Makimedia doesn't do

  • Provide, sell or rent any films or shows
  • Host video or stream anything over the internet
  • Find, download or share videos for you
  • Connect to Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or any other streaming service

Only play and stream videos you have the right to use. Film and show titles, posters and artwork seen in the app and in the screenshots on this site belong to their owners and are shown only to illustrate how Makimedia displays a library. Read the legal notice.
